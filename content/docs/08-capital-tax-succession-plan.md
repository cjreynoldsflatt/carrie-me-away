## Purpose

This guide explains the economics with examples. It does not replace the operating agreement, service agreement, or CPA tax schedule.

## 1. Starting Position

| Member | Capital | Governance | Residual interest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Carrie | approximately $500,000 | 51% | 51% |
| Cameron | $0 | 49% | 49% Service-Based Profits Interest |

Carrie's capital remains hers. Cameron's 49% relates to future residual economics above the protected capital arrangement.

## 2. Cameron's 49% and Reverse Vesting

Cameron holds the full 49% from the Grant Date while performing his duties.

Five-year reverse vesting makes 9.8 percentage points nonforfeitable each year.

If Cameron voluntarily stops after Year 3:

- 29.4% remains Cameron's permanent residual interest;
- 19.6% is reallocated to Carrie;
- governance remains 51% / 49% unless amended.

Doc 09 controls exceptions and acceleration.

## 3. Operating Profit

Assume CMA-I has $40,000 of distributable operating profit while Cameron's full interest is outstanding:

| Member | Percentage | Distribution |
|---|---:|---:|
| Carrie | 51% | $20,400 |
| Cameron | 49% | $19,600 |

The capital hurdle is not repaid before every ordinary rental-profit distribution.

## 4. Capital Proceeds

Capital proceeds follow a different order:

```text
outside obligations
-> applicable Member Loans
-> attributed unrecovered capital
-> remaining residual value
```

## 5. Sale Above Capital

Assume:

- Carrie has $200,000 of capital attributed to Flamingo;
- Flamingo produces $325,000 of net capital proceeds after outside obligations;
- no Member Loan remains;
- Cameron's full 49% is outstanding.

```text
$325,000
- 200,000 return of Carrie's capital
= 125,000 residual
```

| Member | Capital return | Residual | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Carrie | $200,000 | $63,750 | $263,750 |
| Cameron | $0 | $61,250 | $61,250 |

## 6. Sale Below Capital

Assume Carrie's attributed capital is $200,000 but net proceeds are $160,000.

- all $160,000 is applied to Carrie's capital;
- there is no residual sale distribution;
- $40,000 remains part of Carrie's Company-level unrecovered capital unless later returned, finally written off, or lost on final liquidation;
- Cameron does not owe Carrie the $40,000.

Ordinary operating profit from other properties may still be distributed under the residual percentages unless CMA-I retains it for business needs. Later capital proceeds may be designated to restore Company-level unrecovered capital under Doc 02.

## 7. Future Capital

If Carrie later contributes another $100,000 as capital, her Unrecovered Contributed Capital increases by $100,000.

If Cameron later contributes $50,000 as capital, Cameron obtains a separate $50,000 capital balance. His cash capital is separate from his Service-Based Profits Interest.

## 8. Member Loans

An emergency advance defaults to a Member Loan unless both members designate it as capital.

A Member Loan is repaid according to its note and generally before capital returns and residual distributions, subject to outside debt, law, and reserves.

## 9. Refinance

Refinance proceeds do not automatically reduce capital.

- If retained or reinvested, capital remains unrecovered.
- If distributed and expressly designated as return of capital, the recipient's capital balance is reduced.
- If used to repay a Member Loan, it is loan repayment.

## 10. Taxable Income Is Not Cash

A member may receive taxable income on a K-1 without receiving the same amount of cash.

CMA-I therefore plans a separate tax distribution, usually targeted for March 15, under Docs 06 and 21.

Each member remains responsible for personal tax payments.

## 11. Tax Records

CMA-I tracks three different concepts:

- contractual Unrecovered Contributed Capital;
- Section 704(b)-oriented tax capital;
- outside tax basis.

They are not interchangeable.

## 12. Profits-Interest Tax Implementation

Before the Grant Date, the CPA/tax attorney should confirm:

- the capital hurdle and $0 current liquidation value for Cameron;
- owner treatment from the Grant Date;
- the allocation method;
- no deduction for the grant's value;
- Section 83(b) filing decision;
- Section 2701 and gift-tax treatment;
- Section 1061 applicability;
- reporting and annual record requirements.

Doc 21 is the implementation checklist.

## 13. Succession

### Carrie dies

Carrie's governance, capital, and residual rights pass to Jay Flatt.

### Cameron dies

Cameron's remaining forfeitable interest first accelerates. His full governance and economic interest passes to Jay Flatt.

Jay is admitted as substitute member after the transfer is legally effective and the required documents are delivered.

If Jay cannot take the interest, the controlling estate documents and applicable law govern.

## 14. What This Guide Does Not Decide

This guide does not determine:

- estate-tax or basis consequences;
- final K-1 allocations;
- Section 754 treatment;
- lender consent;
- appraisal value;
- final legal transfer mechanics.

Those items are handled under Docs 02, 21, and professional advice.
