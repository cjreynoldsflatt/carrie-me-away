# 21 — CMA Tax & Capital Implementation Schedule

**Status:** Canonical planning draft  
**Version:** 2026-08-26.2  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-26

## Purpose

This document converts the capital / profits-interest design in Docs 02, 06, 08, and 09 into an implementation checklist.

It does not replace the tax professional's final allocations or filings.

## 1. Before Cameron's Grant Date

- confirm CMA-I partnership tax classification;
- document Carrie's capital amount actually funded;
- establish Carrie's protected unrecovered capital and liquidation hurdle;
- confirm Cameron has **$0 current liquidation value** from Carrie's existing capital immediately before the grant;
- finalize the Service-Based Profits Interest terms and five-year reverse vesting;
- obtain tax / valuation review addressing the profits-interest rules and family-transfer / Section 2701 concern;
- confirm the operating agreement's allocation / liquidation provisions match the intended economics.

## 2. Grant Closing

Recommended sequence:

```text
Carrie capital funded / fixed
        ↓
Capital hurdle documented
        ↓
Profits-interest valuation / tax memo
        ↓
Cameron profits-interest grant effective
        ↓
Partner treatment begins on Grant Date
        ↓
Company / member records updated
```

On the Grant Date:

- execute Doc 09 / related grant consent;
- record 49% residual profits interest;
- record governance separately at Carrie 51% / Cameron 49%;
- establish the vesting calendar;
- establish Cameron as a partner for tax administration consistent with the intended safe-harbor treatment;
- do not book the grant as a claim on Carrie's existing protected capital.

## 3. Within 30 Days After Grant

Rev. Proc. 2001-43 states that taxpayers meeting its conditions need not file a Section 83(b) election. CMA's planning rule is nevertheless to **prepare a protective Section 83(b) election unless tax counsel specifically advises not to file it**.

If counsel directs filing, document timely filing and retain proof permanently with the grant records.

## 4. Capital Accounting

Maintain separate concepts:

### Contractual Unrecovered Capital
Tracks contributed capital economically owed back under CMA's capital hurdle.

### Section 704(b)-Oriented Capital Account
Maintained for partnership tax/economic-allocation purposes.

### Outside Tax Basis
Tracked for each partner for federal tax purposes.

Do not treat these three numbers as interchangeable.

## 5. Additional Funding

Every member advance must be documented at or before funding as either:

- additional capital; or
- member loan.

Emergency / short-term advances default to member loans unless both members approve capital treatment.

For a member loan, document:

- principal;
- date;
- interest rate;
- maturity / repayment terms;
- lender subordination if required;
- repayments.

## 6. Annual Partnership Tax Cycle

Each year:

- close books and property subledgers;
- provide the CPA with capital, member-loan, debt, fixed-asset, sale/refi, and distribution records;
- update Section 704(b)-oriented capital schedules;
- update each member's basis records;
- allocate partnership items consistent with the economic arrangement and tax rules;
- issue Schedule K-1s;
- determine annual BBA election-out eligibility;
- if eligible and approved, make the election on the timely Form 1065 and required schedule;
- if not electing out, confirm partnership representative and designated individual;
- review state filing obligations.

## 7. Partnership Representative

If CMA-I validly elects out of the BBA regime for a tax year, no BBA partnership representative is needed for that year.

If CMA-I does not elect out:

- CMA-I is the partnership representative;
- Cameron is the intended designated individual while eligible and willing to serve;
- actions with material economic impact should follow the internal approval rules in Doc 02 even though federal law gives the partnership representative broad authority against the IRS.

## 8. Tax Distributions

Planning target: **March 15**.

Formula:

```text
Member allocated taxable income
× approved Assumed Tax Rate
− prior tax distributions for that tax year
= target tax distribution
```

If no rate is approved by January 31, use **40%** as the internal planning rate only.

Tax distributions remain subject to available cash, lender restrictions, legal obligations, solvency, and reserves.

True up after final K-1s when practical.

## 9. Property Acquisition

For each acquisition, retain for tax accounting:

- settlement statement;
- purchase contract;
- lender closing statement;
- inspection / due-diligence costs as relevant;
- initial repairs / improvements separated where needed;
- allocation of acquisition costs;
- placed-in-service date;
- depreciation schedule inputs.

## 10. Refinance

Record:

- new loan proceeds;
- payoff of old debt;
- closing costs;
- cash retained / distributed;
- property and member-level basis consequences for CPA review;
- any member loan repayment.

Do not treat refinance proceeds automatically as taxable profit or return of member capital without the tax/economic analysis.

## 11. Property Sale

Before distributing sale proceeds:

1. close the sale and reserve for obligations;
2. calculate applicable unrecovered capital;
3. apply the contractual capital hurdle;
4. calculate residual economics;
5. provide closing / depreciation / improvement records to CPA;
6. calculate gain, depreciation recapture, and member allocations;
7. preserve cash for tax / legal obligations before final distribution.

## 12. Death / Sale / Transfer of a CMA-I Interest

When a membership interest transfers by death, sale, or other qualifying event:

- obtain the transfer-date valuation / basis documentation;
- update substitute-member records;
- review mandatory basis-adjustment rules that may apply;
- review whether a Section 754 election is available and beneficial;
- if making a Section 754 election, attach the required statement to the timely partnership return for the transfer year;
- remember that a Section 754 election generally continues for later years unless properly revoked.

## 13. Annual Tax / Capital Sign-Off

Maintain an annual checklist signed or acknowledged by the responsible preparer confirming:

- [ ] contributions reconciled
- [ ] member loans reconciled
- [ ] unrecovered capital reconciled
- [ ] distributions reconciled
- [ ] Cameron vesting / forfeiture status confirmed
- [ ] tax capital accounts updated
- [ ] outside basis schedules updated
- [ ] property basis / depreciation updated
- [ ] BBA election reviewed
- [ ] partnership representative reviewed if needed
- [ ] tax distributions calculated
- [ ] state filings reviewed
- [ ] transfers / Section 754 events reviewed
