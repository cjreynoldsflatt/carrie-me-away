If counsel directs filing, file within the applicable deadline and retain proof permanently.

## 4. Capital Definitions

### Unrecovered Contributed Capital

```text
written capital contributions
+ later advances expressly designated or reclassified as capital
- distributions expressly designated as return of capital
= member's Unrecovered Contributed Capital
```

Ordinary profit distributions, depreciation, tax allocations, and book losses do not automatically reduce this contractual balance.

### Attributed Property Capital

For each property subledger:

```text
approved purchase equity
+ approved acquisition closing costs
+ initial reserve funded as capital
+ approved capital improvements
+ debt principal funded directly from CMA-I capital
+ operating shortfalls expressly designated as capital
+ other amounts approved by written resolution
- prior amounts expressly returned from that property as capital
= Attributed Property Capital
```

Exclude:

- ordinary operating expenses paid from property operations;
- Member Loans;
- depreciation and other noncash tax items;
- amounts already returned as capital.

Shared portfolio costs remain at CMA-I or are allocated using a reasonable, consistently applied method approved in writing.

### Section 704(b) Capital and Outside Basis

Maintain Section 704(b)-oriented capital accounts and each member's outside tax basis separately from contractual capital balances.

## 5. Member Loans and Additional Funding

Every advance must be designated as:

- additional capital; or
- Member Loan.

Emergency or short-term advances default to Member Loans unless both members approve capital treatment.

A Member Loan record includes principal, date, purpose, interest, maturity, payment terms, and subordination.

Multiple Member Loans have equal priority pro rata by unpaid principal unless their written terms establish another priority.

## 6. Capital-Transaction Waterfall

For a sale, distributed refinance, subsidiary sale, liquidation, or other designated Capital Transaction:

```text
1. outside debt, taxes, transaction costs, and required reserves
2. applicable property- or transaction-specific Member Loans and accrued interest
3. Attributed Property Capital, pro rata among contributing members unless a resolution establishes another priority
4. other CMA-I Unrecovered Contributed Capital if the resolution designates a broader capital return
5. remaining residual value under the current residual percentages
```

If proceeds do not satisfy applicable capital, no residual value is distributed. Unpaid capital remains unrecovered at CMA-I unless a final liquidation or approved write-off determines otherwise.

Refinance proceeds reduce capital only when actually distributed and expressly designated as return of capital.

## 7. Annual Partnership Tax Cycle

Each year:

- close books and property subledgers;
- reconcile contributions, Member Loans, capital returns, and distributions;
- provide the CPA debt, fixed-asset, sale, refinance, and property records;
- update Section 704(b)-oriented capital accounts and outside basis;
- allocate items under the final CPA-approved schedule;
- issue Schedule K-1s;
- review BBA election-out eligibility;
- confirm the Partnership Representative if no election is made;
- review state filings;
- review Section 754 events;
- review Section 1061 applicability and reporting.

## 8. Section 1061 Review

Because Cameron's interest is issued for services and CMA-I invests in rental or investment real estate, the CPA or tax attorney should determine annually whether any interest is an Applicable Partnership Interest and whether Section 1061 recharacterizes any long-term capital gain.

Retain the analysis and any required partner or partnership reporting.

## 9. Partnership Representative

If CMA-I validly elects out of the centralized partnership audit regime, no BBA Partnership Representative is needed for that year.

If CMA-I does not elect out:

- designate the Partnership Representative and, when required, a designated individual;
- document the internal approval expectations in Doc 02;
- recognize that federal law may give the representative authority to bind CMA-I before the IRS.

## 10. Tax Distributions

Planning target: **March 15**.

```text
member's allocated taxable income
× approved Assumed Tax Rate
- prior tax distributions for that tax year
= target tax distribution
```

If no rate is approved by January 31, use 40% as an internal planning rate only.

Tax distributions remain subject to cash, reserves, debt restrictions, law, and solvency. True up after final K-1s when practical.

## 11. Property Acquisition and Depreciation

Retain:

- settlement statement and purchase contract;
- lender closing statement;
- acquisition and due-diligence costs;
- initial repair and improvement detail;
- allocation of acquisition costs;
- placed-in-service date;
- depreciation inputs;
- cost-segregation decision if considered.

## 12. Refinance

Record:

- new proceeds;
- old-debt payoff;
- closing costs;
- cash retained or distributed;
- capital-return designation;
- Member Loan repayment;
- basis and debt-allocation consequences.

Do not automatically treat refinance proceeds as profit or capital return.

## 13. Property Sale

Before final distribution:

1. close the sale and reserve for obligations;
2. calculate Attributed Property Capital;
3. apply the waterfall;
4. provide closing, depreciation, and improvement records to the CPA;
5. calculate gain, recapture, Section 1061 consequences, and allocations;
6. reserve for tax and legal obligations;
7. approve the distribution by resolution.

## 14. Transfer, Death, or Sale of a CMA-I Interest

- obtain transfer-date valuation and basis records;
- update substitute-member and cap-table records;
- review mandatory basis-adjustment rules;
- review a Section 754 election and related Section 743(b) or 734(b) adjustment;
- retain the election statement and schedules if made;
- update capital, basis, debt, and successor information.
