# 23 — CMA Company Resolutions & Annual Consents

**Status:** Canonical planning draft / template library  
**Version:** 2026-08-26.2  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-26

## Purpose

Provide a simple paper trail for company decisions that should not live only in email, text, or memory.

Use the correct entity name for every resolution.

## 1. Annual Consent - CMA-I

Record at least annually:

- Carrie 51% governance / Cameron 49% governance confirmed;
- current residual economic percentages confirmed;
- Cameron vesting / forfeiture status confirmed;
- member capital and member loans reconciled;
- authorized bank signers confirmed;
- BBA election / partnership-representative review completed;
- tax-distribution planning reviewed;
- insurance review completed;
- annual compliance calendar reviewed;
- data-access / backup-operator matrix reviewed;
- current Property LLC list confirmed.

## 2. Annual Consent - CMA-PM

Confirm:

- authorized representatives;
- CMA-PM company working-capital target;
- operating bank;
- Maryland security-deposit bank;
- DoorLoop / Super / Google / Voice stack;
- common-ownership management / collection filing status;
- insurance decisions;
- vendor-control standards;
- backup operator;
- tenant screening criteria baseline.

## 3. Property Acquisition Resolution

Template:

```text
RESOLVED, that [ENTITY] is authorized to acquire [PROPERTY ADDRESS]
for a purchase price up to $[AMOUNT], subject to the approved financing,
due-diligence, insurance, reserve, and closing conditions.

RESOLVED, that [AUTHORIZED PERSON] may execute documents reasonably
necessary to complete the approved transaction within those limits.
```

Attach:

- property analysis;
- final purchase terms;
- financing summary;
- reserve target;
- due-diligence checklist;
- insurance binder.

## 4. Property Sale Resolution

Record:

- Property LLC / address;
- approved listing / minimum acceptable terms if applicable;
- tenant / ROFR compliance confirmation;
- expected debt payoff;
- unrecovered capital attributable to the property;
- estimated residual waterfall;
- authorized signer.

## 5. Member Loan Resolution

```text
Lender Member: [NAME]
Borrower: CMA-I / CMA-PM / [PROPERTY LLC]
Principal: $[AMOUNT]
Date: [DATE]
Interest Rate: [RATE]
Repayment Terms: [TERMS]
Subordination: [IF ANY]
Purpose: [PURPOSE]
```

Confirm the advance is a **loan, not capital**.

## 6. Additional Capital Resolution

Record:

- contributing member;
- amount;
- date;
- capital attribution;
- impact on unrecovered capital;
- confirmation that governance / residual economics do not change unless separately amended.

## 7. Profits-Interest Grant Resolution

Attach / confirm:

- Grant Date;
- Cameron 49% Service-Based Profits Interest;
- $0 current liquidation value from Carrie's preexisting protected capital;
- five-year reverse-vesting schedule;
- partner treatment from Grant Date;
- tax / valuation memo;
- protective Section 83(b) decision / filing evidence if applicable.

## 8. Annual Vesting Confirmation

```text
Grant Date: [DATE]
Completed Service Year: [NUMBER]
Nonforfeitable Portion: [__%]
Forfeitable Portion: [__%]
Service Status: Continuing / Leave / Disability / Other
Forfeiture Triggered? Yes / No
Notes: [NOTES]
```

## 9. Distribution Resolution

Record:

- distributable cash;
- required reserves;
- outstanding member loans;
- tax distributions already made;
- current residual percentages;
- amount approved for Carrie;
- amount approved for Cameron;
- whether any amount is return of capital rather than profit distribution.

## 10. Bank Authorization Resolution

For each bank account record:

- legal account owner;
- account purpose;
- authorized signers / users;
- transaction limits if any;
- whether dual approval is required by bank configuration;
- security-deposit restriction if applicable.

Never include passwords or recovery codes in the resolution.

## 11. Insurance Review Resolution

Annually record that Doc 20 was reviewed and identify:

- coverages renewed;
- coverages declined;
- material limit / deductible changes;
- broker recommendations requiring follow-up.

## 12. Annual Compliance Certification

At year end or beginning of the next year, record whether:

- Maryland entity filings are current;
- local rental licenses are current;
- lead compliance is current where applicable;
- current lease materials are in use;
- insurance is current;
- security-deposit account is reconciled;
- property reserves and CMA-PM working capital are reviewed;
- material unresolved compliance items are listed with an owner and due date.
